# Spanish course: A2 to C2 (#3)

Date: 2026-10-17
Issue: https://github.com/ozerozdas/sprigo/issues/3

## Goal

Learners who finish Spanish A1 get an A2 level. Content only: one new `A2:` block in `courses/es.yml`; the AI writes exercises and the guidebook from it.

## Decisions

- **Same shape as A1:** 10 units × 4 steps and an `A2 Checkpoint`. The app, chests and tests then work unchanged, and #4–#6 (fr, de, tr) can copy the shape.
- **Guidebooks need no YAML:** `loadGuide` (`src/lessons.ts`) builds each unit's guide from its vocabulary and grammar.
- **Language:** titles and descriptions in English, `vocabulary` and `grammar.pattern` in Spanish, as in A1.
- **Grammar is Spanish A2, not translated English A2:** pretérito perfecto, imperfecto vs indefinido, futuro simple, object pronouns, *doler*, tú imperative, *hay que / tener que*.
- **Step ids** are unique across the file (no clash with A1 ids).
- **Activity mix** follows A1: every step has 4–6 activities, each unit ends with a `dialogue` step where it fits.

## Units

1. Jobs & Work: professions (*Soy enfermera*), at work (*trabajar como/en*), frequency adverbs, job interview (*desde*, *hace … que*)
2. Comparisons: *más/menos … que*, *mejor/peor/mayor/menor*, *tan … como / tanto como*, superlative (*el más … de*)
3. At the Shops: quantities, demonstratives *este/ese/aquel*, direct object pronouns (*Me lo llevo*), returns
4. Health & the Body: body parts, *doler*, at the doctor, advice (*deberías*, tú imperative)
5. Travel Plans: travel words, *ir a* + infinitive, airport/station, hotel booking (*quería*, *me gustaría*)
6. The Future: futuro simple regular, irregular (*tendré, haré*), *si* + presente + futuro, plans (*pensar/esperar* + infinitive)
7. When I Was a Child: imperfecto regular, *ser/ir/ver*, habits (*soler*), describing the past
8. Telling Stories: indefinido vs imperfecto, *estaba* + gerundio interrupted, connectors (*de repente, mientras*), telling a story
9. Life Experiences: pretérito perfecto, irregular participles, *ya / todavía no / alguna vez / nunca*, talking about experiences
10. Invitations & Rules: phone and messages, invitations (*¿Te apetece…?*), permission (*¿Puedo…? ¿Te importa si…?*), rules (*hay que, no se puede*)

Checkpoint: like A1's, plus `error_correct`.

## B1–C2 (follow-up, same PR)

The user asked to finish Spanish up to C2 in the same PR. B1–C2 keep the shape (10 units × 4 steps, checkpoint with `error_correct`). Topics follow en.yml's B1–C2 units; the grammar spine is Spanish: subjunctive (B1 presente, B2 imperfecto and time/purpose clauses, C1 relative clauses, *como si*, *por mucho que*), probability future/conditional, *se* passives, periphrases, verbs of change, register and regional varieties at C2.

- B1: Past Tenses Together, Wishes & Feelings, Opinions, Advice & Requests, Hypotheses, Reported Speech, Relative Clauses, Work & Careers, Habits & Lifestyle, Media & Technology
- B2: Regrets & Hypotheticals, Deduction & Speculation, Passive & Impersonal, Time Clauses & the Future, Linking Ideas, Purpose & Conditions, Advanced Reported Speech, Debate & Persuasion, Society & the Environment, Science, Culture & the Arts
- C1: Subjunctive Mastery, Emphasis & Word Order, Advanced Structures, Hedging & Discourse, Professional Life, Media & Information, Science, Technology & Ethics, Society & Politics, Idioms & Colloquial Spanish, Argument & Academic Writing
- C2: Register & Nuance, Modality & Stance, Figurative Language, Rhetoric & Persuasion, Humour, Irony & Wordplay, Academic & Formal Writing, Literature & Style, Ideas & Philosophy, Culture, Language & Identity, Mastery & Mediation

## Tests

`src/course.test.ts`: the A1-only loop keeps checking fr/de/tr; es gets its own check: range `A1–C2`, every level 10 × 4 with a checkpoint, step ids unique, every step has vocabulary or grammar. Written so fr/de/tr move into it by adding their iso.

## Verification

`pnpm test`; `pnpm eval` on a few A2 steps with Ollama (temporary local case, not committed).

## Out of scope

A2+ for fr/de/tr (#4–#6).
